About Felma Cakes

The cake side of life, from Abuakwa, Kumasi.

Every cake tells a story

Felma Cakes is passionate about making celebrations sweeter and more memorable. From simple birthday cakes to elegant wedding cakes, every design is prepared with care, creativity and love.

For all your cake and pastry needs, Felma Cakes is a one-stop place for treats made fresh and made your way.

Learn cake baking with Felma

Felma Cakes teaches cake baking, cake decoration and the art of making a variety of pastries, with the aim of bringing out the best in every student.

Registration is by phone or WhatsApp, and new classes start throughout the year. Ask us when the next one begins.
