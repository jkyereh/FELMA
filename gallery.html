How to order

  1. Send your idea

    Tell us the occasion, the date you need the cake and any colours, themes or pictures you like.

  2. Agree the details

    We confirm the design, size and flavour with you, and share the price.

  3. Collect your cake

    Your cake is made fresh for your date and ready when you need it.

Have these ready when you message us

  • The date you need the cake
  • How many people it should serve
  • Your preferred flavour
  • Colours, theme or a reference picture
  • Whether you will collect it or need delivery
